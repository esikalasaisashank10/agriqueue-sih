Without scheduled capacity, every farmer rushes at dawn out of fear of missing the weighbridge quota. Tractors back up onto state highways, tempers flare, and grains sit exposed in the open.

Step 01

Capacity-locked time bands

Each center declares hourly throughput (e.g. 14 trolleys/hour). Slots close strictly when capacity is exhausted to guarantee no over-booking.

Real-time collision & waiting list
